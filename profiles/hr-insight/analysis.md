# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the value of long-form recruiting, talent, and HR analysis for readers who follow recruiting, talent acquisition, the labor and talent market, HR technology, and employment policy, with particular interest in Korea and in how AI is changing hiring and talent search.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Essential.** Original research or analysis with strong evidence that changes how one should think about hiring, talent markets, or recruiting technology.
- **7-8: High value.** Insightful, data-backed or experience-backed pieces with a clear argument and practical relevance to recruiting and talent decisions.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Reasonable analysis with some new angle, useful frameworks, or practitioner cases.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Generic advice, listicles, recycled ideas, or thinly disguised marketing.
- **0-2: Noise.** Pure promotion, webinar invitations, or off-topic content.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward evidence, specificity, and original thinking. Pieces about AI in recruiting, sourcing, skills-based hiring, talent data, and how hiring managers define and adjust candidate requirements deserve extra weight. General management or leadership content scores low unless it has a clear talent or hiring angle.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
