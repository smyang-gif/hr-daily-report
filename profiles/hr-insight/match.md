# HR research & insight profile

Use this profile for long-form analysis, research reports, newsletters, and practitioner essays about recruiting, talent acquisition, workforce strategy, skills, the future of work, and HR technology.

Typical items include analyst research (Josh Bersin, Aptitude Research, Indeed Hiring Lab), Harvard Business Review or MIT Sloan articles on talent and management, recruiting newsletters such as Recruiting Brainfood and ERE, and data-backed studies of candidate or employee behavior.

Do not use this profile for short timely news (use `hr-news`) or law and policy events (use `hr-policy`).
