# Labor policy & regulation profile

Use this profile for laws, regulations, court rulings, and government policy that affect employment, hiring, wages, working hours, labor relations, and the use of AI or data in hiring, in Korea or in other major jurisdictions.

Typical items include Korean Ministry of Employment and Labor announcements, amendments to the Labor Standards Act or related laws, minimum wage and working-hour decisions, major labor court rulings, union and collective-bargaining developments with broad impact, employment subsidy programs, and AI-hiring or candidate-data regulations (EU AI Act, US state laws, Korean personal information rules).

Do not use this profile for company hiring news, HR tech product news, or opinion essays without a concrete policy event.
