# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of labor and employment policy news for readers who follow recruiting, talent acquisition, the labor and talent market, HR technology, and employment policy, with particular interest in Korea and in how AI is changing hiring and talent search.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major change.** Enacted laws or final decisions that change hiring, employment, or candidate-data rules for many employers (for example a major Labor Standards Act amendment, a new AI-in-hiring regulation taking effect, or a landmark ruling).
- **7-8: High value.** Bills passing key stages, government plans with concrete timelines, important rulings, minimum wage or working-hour decisions, and regulatory guidance employers must act on.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Proposals, consultations, subsidy programs, statistics releases from labor authorities, or notable disputes with possible wider effect.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine ministry events, ceremonial announcements, local programs, or political statements without concrete content.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic, purely partisan commentary, or duplicated press releases with no new facts.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward items that tell an employer or recruiter what changes, from when, and for whom. Prefer primary sources and specific dates and thresholds. Rules affecting hiring processes, candidate data, AI assessment, and employment contracts deserve extra weight. Do not reward alarmist headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
