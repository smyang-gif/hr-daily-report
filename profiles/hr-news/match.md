# HR & recruiting news profile

Use this profile for timely developments in recruiting, talent acquisition, the labor and talent market, workforce trends, and the HR technology industry, in Korea or globally.

Typical items include hiring and layoff announcements by notable companies, employment statistics and hiring-demand data, HR tech and recruiting-tool launches, funding, M&A and earnings of HR tech or staffing companies, changes in how companies recruit, assess, or source talent, AI adoption in hiring, and notable shifts in jobseeker or employee behavior.

Do not use this profile for labor law and government policy (use `hr-policy`), long-form opinion or research essays (use `hr-insight`), individual job postings, routine corporate PR, celebrity or sports transfers, or general business news without a workforce angle.
