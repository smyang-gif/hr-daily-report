# AI research paper profile

Use this profile for academic preprints and papers on artificial intelligence, machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, reinforcement learning, and the systems that train or serve such models.

Typical items include arXiv preprints presenting a new method, architecture, training recipe, benchmark, dataset, evaluation, or empirical study, along with position papers and survey papers that consolidate a field.

Do not use this profile for news coverage *about* research, product or funding announcements, blog posts, or tutorials — even when they discuss a paper. Those belong to the news or blog profiles. Route the paper itself here and the coverage there.
